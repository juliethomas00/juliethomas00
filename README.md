## Hi there 👋

My name is Julie and welcome to my GitHub page!

As a Master's of Marine Biology student at James Cook University, I am always learning how to use new and exciting tools and advancing my skills in data analysis and visualization. This platform will become a collage of all the cool projects I get the opportunity to work on!

Throughout my career, I aspire to contribute to climate change resilience and conservation at a human scale. I am particularly interested in evidence-based policymaking, knowledge co-production, stakeholder engagement, community outreach, and translational ecology. I am especially passionate about ensuring that conservation and management initiatives explicitly consider local livelihoods and human rights alongside environmental objectives. Ultimately, I hope to work in a multidisciplinary space that promotes collaboration between scientists, communities, and decision-makers to improve both ecological and human well-being, as I strongly believe that neither can be successful without the other. 

Follow along on my journey (from behind the scenes)!
